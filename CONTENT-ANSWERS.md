## A. Booking and payment

1. Advance ✏️ → A part advance by UPI confirms the booking (about 20% of the quote). The balance is paid at the end of the trip.
2. Payment methods ✏️ → UPI, cash and bank transfer. No cards.
3. Included in a quote ✏️ (same as your taxi site)
   - Fuel: included
   - Driver charges: included
   - Tolls: extra, paid by the customer
   - Parking: extra, paid by the customer
   - State entry tax (outstation): extra, paid by the customer
   - Entry tickets, boat rides, cruises, jeep safari: not included
   - Driver allowance on overnight trips: extra
   - Night charges (driving between 10 PM and 6 AM): extra
4. Cancellation charges ✏️ → Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Less than 24 hours before pickup, or a no-show: the advance is not refunded, because the vehicle is blocked for the day.
   (A tempo blocks a whole day, so this is simpler and fairer than the taxi site's 12-hour / 4-hour rule. If your brother prefers the taxi-site rule, write it here instead.)
5. Advance refund ✏️ → Yes, in full, if cancelled 24 hours or more before pickup.
6. Refund method and time ✏️ → Back to the same UPI or bank account within 5–7 working days (same as taxi site).
7. If we have to cancel ✏️ → The customer chooses: a full refund of the advance, or a replacement vehicle.
8. No-shows and late groups ✏️ → The driver waits 30 minutes after the pickup time. After that, the booking can be treated as a no-show and the advance is not refunded. If the group is running late, they should call or WhatsApp, and we'll try to adjust the plan.
9. Partner mini buses ❓ →  "Booking and payment are through us, under the same cancellation policy. We confirm the bus and driver details on WhatsApp before the trip."
10. One-line summary ✅ → Claude Code can write it from points 1–7.
